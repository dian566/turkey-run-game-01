Turkey Run — Escape Thanksgiving Dinner

A turkey, a farmer, a carving knife, and 1500 metres to freedom.

SPACE or CLICK — jump  •  DOWN — slide under knives  •  P — pause  •  M — mute  •  R — restart

Jump the ovens, slide under the carving knives, grab the corn. Every hit lets the farmer catch up — lose all three lives, or let him reach you, and you are Thanksgiving dinner. Reach the forest at 1500 m and you escape. An original game: all art and sound drawn and synthesised in code.
